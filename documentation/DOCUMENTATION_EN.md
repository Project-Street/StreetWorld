# StreetWorld Documentation

[简体中文](DOCUMENTATION_ZH.md)

StreetWorld is a closed-loop autonomous driving simulation platform. An AD policy receives observations such as camera images and vehicle state, then outputs trajectories or control inputs to drive the vehicle in simulation.
